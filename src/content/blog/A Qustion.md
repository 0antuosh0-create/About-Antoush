Hi again, it's me.

I've been wondering about something. Not just today, but for as long as I can remember.

I've always had this weird feeling of sadness. Not the kind of sadness that comes after something bad happens. Just... sadness. Even when everything is okay. Even when I have good days. Even when nothing is wrong. I look around me, and everything seems fine. I should be happy. I know I should be. But I still feel sad.

And every time I try to understand why, I end up in the same place. I don't know. I don't know if this is normal. I don't know if everyone feels this way. I don't know if it's just part of who I am. I don't know if something is wrong with me. I don't know if there's even a reason for it, or if I've just been looking for one because I can't accept that maybe there isn't.

I just... don't know.

And lately I've realized something. "I don't know" isn't just my answer to this question. It's my answer to almost every question in my life.

Who am I, really? I don't know.

Why do I feel like this? I don't know.

Where am I supposed to go? I don't know.

What am I looking for? I don't know.

Will I ever understand any of it?

...I don't know.

For a long time, I thought I had to find an answer to every question, as if, if I searched long enough, one day everything would finally make sense. But maybe that's not how life works. Maybe some questions aren't meant to be answered. Maybe they're just meant to stay with us.

So maybe I don't need to chase every answer anymore. Maybe I just need to keep walking. To walk past the questions instead of wrestling with them. And if one of them stops me and asks, "Well? Do you know now?" I'll probably smile... and quietly answer, "I don't know either."
